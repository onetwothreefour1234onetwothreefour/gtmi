Product Design measures who can even apply. It carries the highest pillar
weight in PAQ — 28% — because the eligibility criteria are the single
biggest determinant of a programme&rsquo;s real-world utility. A programme
that scores well on every other pillar but admits only a narrow professional
slice serves few candidates; a programme with broad eligibility makes the
rest of the architecture genuinely usable.

The pillar combines three sub-factors. Qualification Threshold (50% of
pillar) covers the salary floor as a percentage of local median wage,
minimum education, work experience, language proficiency, and any age
cap. System Design (30%) captures the number of mandatory criteria,
whether the system is compensatory or conjunctive, and how many distinct
qualifying tracks exist. Volume (20%) records whether the programme is
gated by an annual quota. High-scoring programmes combine reasonable
income thresholds, achievable academic and language bars, several entry
routes, and either no quota or a transparently published one.
