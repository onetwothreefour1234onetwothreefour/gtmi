Pathway measures where the visa leads — the clarity and attainability of
progression to permanent residence and citizenship. At 22% of PAQ it
carries the second-highest pillar weight because mobility is rarely a
one-step transaction; the candidate is choosing a future, not just a
current visa. The pillar was simplified in methodology v5.0.0 (ADR-031),
which collapsed it from eleven indicators across three sub-factors to
five indicators across two sub-factors and retired the tax sub-factor
entirely. GTMI no longer measures host-country tax treatment — for that,
candidates should consult the country&rsquo;s tax authority directly.

Two sub-factors. Permanent Residency (40% of pillar) records whether a
PR pathway is available from the visa and the minimum years of residence
required to reach it. Citizenship (60%) records whether a citizenship
pathway exists from this track, the total minimum years from visa entry
to naturalisation, and whether the country permits dual citizenship.
Where a pathway is unavailable, the indicators downstream of that
boolean score zero — absence of a pathway is the worst outcome, not
missing data. High scorers offer a clearly published PR track at five
years or fewer, a credible citizenship route within ten years, and
permit dual citizenship.
