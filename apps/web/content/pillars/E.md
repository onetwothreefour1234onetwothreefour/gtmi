Performance Outcomes measures how reliable the promise is — whether the
rules a candidate plans around today are likely to still be the rules
in three or five years, and whether the programme has the track record
to back that promise. At 15% of PAQ it is the most proprietary pillar,
because no incumbent index measures policy reliability seriously, and
it is one of the clearest commercial differentiators GTMI offers.
Methodology v6.0.0 (ADR-032) simplified it from eight indicators across
three sub-factors to four indicators across two sub-factors, and retired
the World Bank Worldwide Governance Indicators and V-Dem ingestion path
entirely. Every Pillar E value now comes from the same Tier&nbsp;1
government sources that feed the rest of the index.

Two sub-factors. Track Record (50% of pillar) captures the
programme&rsquo;s age in years (capped at twenty in scoring) and the
cumulative count of approvals or active visa holders. Older programmes
with larger holder populations score higher because they carry more
evidence that the contract has been honoured at scale. Rule Stability
(50%) records the severity-weighted count of material policy changes
in the last five years and whether the programme has ever been
suspended or abruptly closed in the last ten. High scorers run mature,
large programmes whose rule-set has not been rewritten on short notice
and which have never been pulled.
