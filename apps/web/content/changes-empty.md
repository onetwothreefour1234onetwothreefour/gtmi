Policy change detection ships in the next release. Once live, every
Tier&nbsp;1 government source we track will be re-scraped weekly.
Detected changes appear here within 24 hours, classified by severity
(minor / material / breaking), with diffs and Wayback-archived snapshots
of the page state before and after the change.

Subscribers receive email alerts on material and breaking changes. The
summary text is generated by an LLM and reviewed by a human before
publication.

The per-programme policy timeline on each program detail page activates
from the same data — the timeline component is already in place,
waiting on the feed.
