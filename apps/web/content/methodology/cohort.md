The 30-country universe is fixed: the top thirty economies on the IMD
World Talent Ranking 2025 Appeal sub-index. Every country with an
Appeal score below the cohort floor (53.08, Oman) is out of scope. The
universe is re-anchored within thirty days of each new IMD release.

Within those countries, GTMI scores 85 talent-based premium mobility
programmes that meet the inclusion criteria: officially published
eligibility, individual (not corporate) sponsor channel, premium tier
(skilled or above), and active intake at the start of the scoring
window. Province-level, canton-level, and emirate-level programmes
qualify on the same terms as national programmes.

CME baselines are re-anchored annually within thirty days of each new
IMD release. Tier&nbsp;1 government sources for PAQ are re-scraped
weekly; detected revisions appear on the changes log within 24 hours,
classified minor / material / breaking. The methodology version is
bumped only when a formal ADR accepts a change; existing scores keep
their version stamp.
